Discounted Travel Voucher vs Cash Discount

The discount lowers the selling price. The voucher adds a separate travel benefit under its specific terms.

Compare actual business cost, customer value, gross-profit impact, close rate, discount frequency, and customer experience.

Discounted Travel Voucher vs Gift Card

Gift cards provide choice and simple monetary value. Travel vouchers provide an experience that may feel more memorable but require more explanation.

Choose based on audience relevance, economics, usability, and campaign objective.

Customer Closing Incentive vs Sales SPIFF

The closing incentive goes to the buyer. The SPIFF goes to the seller or channel participant.

Use the first when customer timing or value is the issue. Use the second when salesperson or partner behavior is the issue.

Purchase Incentive vs Loyalty Program

A purchase incentive influences one defined action. A loyalty program encourages repeated behavior over time.

Define the desired behavior before choosing the program.

Customer Incentive vs Rebate

A rebate returns part of the price after the customer follows the redemption process. A separate incentive adds another benefit to the purchase.

Compare customer effort, cost, perceived value, fulfillment, and the sales objective.

A Five-Question Selection Test

  1. Who should be motivated: customer, salesperson, or existing account?
  2. Which action should change?
  3. What is the actual business cost?
  4. What must the participant do to receive the value?
  5. How will the result connect to revenue and margin?
