How to Train a Sales Team to Present Travel Vouchers

Train a sales team to present discounted travel vouchers with eligibility rules, accurate language, objection diagnosis, role-play, CRM tracking, and coaching.

Schedule a Campaign Consultation

Representatives need more than a product sheet. They need to know where the voucher belongs in the close, which purchases qualify, how to explain the terms, when not to present it, and what to record.

Teach the purpose

The discounted travel voucher adds customer value after the core purchase fits. It can influence timing or differentiate the offer without immediately reducing the base price.

It does not fix poor fit, unaffordability, missing authority, weak value, mistrust, or implementation risk.

Teach the eligibility rules

Cover qualifying transaction, customer, product, purchase threshold, campaign dates, combination rules, exception approval, and inventory or issuance controls.

Teach the accurate explanation

Representatives must explain included travel value, recipient-paid costs, restrictions, availability, deadlines, booking or redemption, changes, cancellation, and support. Provide current terms and a plain-language one-page guide.

Teach objection diagnosis

Use a consistent sequence: acknowledge, clarify, classify, confirm, respond, verify, ask. The voucher belongs primarily in the timing or value-added branch after the other decision conditions are settled.

Role-play common scenarios

Practice:

  • “Your price is too high.”
  • “I need to think about it.”
  • “I want to shop around.”
  • “Can you discount it instead?”
  • Questions about recipient costs or availability.
  • A customer who does not qualify.

Score accuracy, diagnosis, timing, disclosure, and the direct closing question.

Define CRM behavior

Require eligibility, presentation, issuance, objection category, discount, decision, order value, gross profit, cancellation, and revenue fields. If use is not recorded, managers cannot evaluate the program.

Coach from real evidence

Review calls, role-play, stage duration, close rate, discount frequency, cancellations, and representative-level outcomes. Correct inaccurate language immediately.

Certify before independent use

Require each representative to pass a short knowledge check and role-play before presenting the offer without manager support.

Put a manager-controlled system behind the script

Use the sales-team voucher explanation and objection handling framework.
