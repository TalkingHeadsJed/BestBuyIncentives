How to Explain a Discounted Travel Voucher to a Customer

Use a clear sequence to explain a discounted travel voucher: eligibility, included value, recipient costs, restrictions, deadlines, redemption, and support.

Schedule a Campaign Consultation

The customer should understand the voucher well enough to make a decision without relying on assumptions. A clear explanation protects trust, reduces support problems, and helps the offer add value instead of creating confusion.

Present it after the core purchase makes sense

Confirm fit, value, affordability, authority, and the remaining objection first. The voucher is most relevant when timing or desire for additional value remains and the purchase qualifies.

Ask permission

“For qualifying purchases during this campaign, we include a discounted travel voucher. May I show you exactly what it provides, what the recipient pays, and the applicable dates?”

This tells the customer the nature of the offer before adding detail.

Explain the included travel value

Describe the eligible travel category, destination options, nights or components, occupancy, and anything subject to availability. Use the exact current program language.

Explain recipient responsibilities

State required recipient costs, amounts that vary, optional upgrades, transportation, deadlines, registration, booking or redemption steps, and documentation.

Explain restrictions and support

Cover availability, blackout or premium dates, transfer, changes, cancellation, and where the recipient gets help. Give the customer the complete terms.

Check understanding

Ask:

“What questions do you have about what is included, what you would pay, or how the process works?”

Do not ask only, “Does that make sense?” Customers may say yes without understanding.

Return to the decision

Once the terms are clear, ask:

“Does that added value affect your timing?”

If yes, ask whether the customer would like to move forward. If no, return to the real objection rather than increasing pressure.

Give representatives approved materials

Use a one-page summary, current terms, a manager escalation path, and CRM fields for presentation, issuance, objection, discount, and outcome.
