How Dealerships Can Use Discounted Travel Vouchers

Learn how car dealerships can use discounted travel vouchers in eligible vehicle, aged-inventory, service-lane, and dealer-group campaigns.

Schedule a Campaign Consultation

Dealerships can use discounted travel vouchers as a customer-facing added-value offer for defined vehicle or service transactions. The program should support a measurable sales objective and be presented after the core transaction makes sense.

Closing qualified vehicle buyers

When a customer confirms vehicle fit and understands the transaction but timing remains, an eligible voucher may give the buyer another reason to complete the purchase without another immediate price concession.

Aged-inventory campaigns

Create a specific campaign for selected age bands, models, or units. Keep eligibility and dates clear and compare the voucher cost with proposed discounts and expected gross.

Traffic-generation events

Use accurate advertising and a qualifying purchase threshold. Track source, appointment, show, qualified opportunity, presentation, sale, gross, and commission.

Service-lane customers

An offer may support defined customer-pay service promotions or replacement-vehicle conversations where appropriate. Service recommendations must remain based on customer and vehicle needs.

Delivery and referral experience

The voucher can be presented as a documented customer benefit during an eligible delivery. Explain complete terms and provide recipient support information.

Dealer-group standardization

Groups can define common offer terms, manager approval, training, CRM fields, issuance, support, and measurement while comparing performance by store and team.

The presentation sequence

  1. Confirm the vehicle and transaction fit.
  2. Diagnose the remaining obstacle.
  3. Confirm purchase and campaign eligibility.
  4. Call it a discounted travel voucher.
  5. Explain included travel value, recipient costs, restrictions, deadlines, redemption, and support.
  6. Ask whether the added value affects timing.
  7. Ask for the decision and record the outcome.
