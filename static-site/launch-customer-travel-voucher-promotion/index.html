How to Launch a Customer Travel-Voucher Promotion

Launch a discounted travel voucher promotion with defined objectives, economics, terms, eligibility, sales training, fulfillment, tracking, and measurement.

Schedule a Campaign Consultation

A successful discounted travel voucher promotion gives customers an exciting reason to choose now and gives your sales team a powerful alternative to price-cutting—with a clear route to measurable revenue.

Define one primary objective

Choose the main goal: generate appointments, improve qualified close rate, protect price, increase average order value, shorten cycle time, move a category, or support referrals. Secondary metrics matter, but one objective guides design.

Establish the baseline

Record current eligible opportunity volume, close rate, discount frequency, order value, gross profit, cycle time, cancellations, and revenue. Without a baseline or control, campaign lift is difficult to establish.

Select and review the voucher

Select a program that fits the customer, makes the travel value easy to communicate, and supports the campaign economics.

Write campaign rules

Define a focused campaign: dates, qualifying customer and transaction, products, threshold, locations, issuance, and CRM fields.

Prepare customer materials

Create engaging promotion copy, a polished one-page overview, recipient instructions, and quick answers that keep the customer experience moving.

Train and certify the team

Teach where the voucher fits, how to diagnose objections, how to present its travel value, how to close, and what to record. Role-play before launch.

Test fulfillment

Walk through the customer journey from eligibility to issuance and redemption, then make the handoff feel effortless for the customer.

Launch in a controlled segment

Start with a defined team, location, product, or period when possible. Review use and outcomes daily during the initial phase.

Measure and decide

Compare qualified close rate, margin, discounts, order value, cycle time, customer experience, attributable revenue, and commission. Use the results to refine and scale the campaign.
