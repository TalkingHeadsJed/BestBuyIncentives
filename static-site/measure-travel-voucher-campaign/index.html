How to Measure a Travel-Voucher Campaign

Measure a discounted travel voucher campaign with eligibility, presentation, close rate, discounts, order value, margin, cycle time, revenue, and commission.

Schedule a Campaign Consultation

Voucher issuance is the beginning of the story. Measure the campaign from eligible opportunity through presentation, decision, gross profit, revenue, commission, and customer experience to see the sales impact clearly.

Define the unit of analysis

Decide whether results will be measured per lead, consultation, qualified opportunity, transaction, representative, location, product, or campaign. Qualified opportunity is often the clearest denominator for closing performance.

Establish a baseline or control

Use a comparable prior period, location, team, product, or randomized eligible group when practical. Account for seasonality, pricing, media, inventory, staffing, and other changes.

Record exposure

For every eligible opportunity, record whether the discounted travel voucher was presented, when, by whom, whether it was issued, and whether it replaced a discount. This shows managers whether the exciting value-add is actually helping reps win more business while protecting margin.

Track funnel outcomes

Measure:

  • Eligible and qualified opportunities.
  • Voucher presentation and issuance rates.
  • Qualified close rate.
  • Median sales-cycle length.
  • Average order value.
  • Discount frequency and amount.
  • Gross profit per opportunity and transaction.
  • Customer experience and successful recipient engagement.
  • Attributable pipeline, closed revenue, and commission.

Calculate incremental economics

Estimate incremental closed transactions and gross profit relative to the baseline or control. Subtract voucher purchase, setup, training, fulfillment, administration, support, and other campaign costs.

Use the baseline to identify the incremental lift your team created.

Segment the result

Compare representative, location, product, deal-size band, objection category, presentation timing, and lead source to identify the strongest expansion opportunities.

Review customer experience

Track activation or redemption where relevant, customer questions, and support responsiveness. A great recipient experience turns the offer into lasting goodwill.

Decide what happens next

Expand the campaign where it produces profitable lift and a strong customer experience; refine training, eligibility, or presentation to unlock more of that potential.

Connect campaign activity to commission revenue
