Sales Promotion Metrics That Matter to Management

Track sales promotion metrics from exposure and qualified conversion through gross profit, incremental revenue, customer outcomes, and commission.

Schedule a Campaign Consultation

Impressions, clicks, and issued incentives describe activity. Management needs to know whether the promotion created qualified demand, protected margin, generated incremental gross profit, and produced acceptable customer outcomes.

Reach and response

Track eligible audience, impressions where relevant, landing-page visits, inquiries, playbook requests, appointment bookings, and completed consultations. Use source, landing page, campaign, and content identifiers.

Qualification

Measure the percentage of responses that become qualified opportunities. A campaign that produces many low-fit leads can burden sales without creating pipeline.

Offer exposure

Record which eligible customers were presented the promotion, when, by whom, and whether the benefit was issued. Exposure is essential for evaluating effect.

Sales outcomes

Track qualified close rate, average order value, product mix, discount frequency and amount, median sales-cycle length, gross profit per opportunity, and cancellation or refund rate.

Financial outcomes

Report attributable pipeline, closed revenue, incremental gross profit, total campaign cost, ROI, and commission revenue. Distinguish total campaign revenue from incremental revenue.

Customer outcomes

Monitor support contacts, complaints, fulfillment, activation or redemption where relevant, satisfaction, referrals, and repeat behavior. A promotion that harms trust creates future cost.

Operational quality

Track representative training completion, eligibility compliance, accurate presentation, issuance errors, support response, and CRM completeness.

Segment the dashboard

Break performance down by source, location, representative, product, deal-size band, customer segment, offer, and objection category. Protect privacy and avoid conclusions from very small samples.

Use a management decision cadence

Review leading indicators weekly and mature financial outcomes monthly or by completed cohort. Every review should produce an action: expand, revise, coach, investigate, or stop.
