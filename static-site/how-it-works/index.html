The program connects three experiences: the manager's campaign, the salesperson's conversation, and the recipient's redemption.

Each must support what was promised.

Plan Your Campaign

Step 1: Define the Business Result

We begin with the behavior or outcome you want to influence:

  • Close more qualified opportunities.
  • Reduce unnecessary discounting.
  • Support appointments or showroom traffic.
  • Move selected inventory.
  • Encourage upgrades or referrals.
  • Improve retention or customer appreciation.

The objective determines the audience, voucher, rules, presentation, and measurement.

Step 2: Review the Sales Economics

Consider:

  • Average transaction value.
  • Gross profit.
  • Typical discount amount and frequency.
  • Current close rate.
  • Sales-cycle length.
  • Size of the eligible opportunity pool.
  • Actual voucher and campaign cost.

This establishes what improvement is required for the program to make economic sense.

Step 3: Select the Discounted Travel Voucher

Review the specific product's:

  • Included benefit.
  • Recipient costs.
  • Destinations or travel options.
  • Occupancy and duration.
  • Activation and travel deadlines.
  • Availability and booking process.
  • Restrictions, cancellation, and transfer rules.
  • Fulfillment and customer support.

Step 4: Design the Campaign

Define eligible products, customers, dates, locations, combination rules, cancellation treatment, sales materials, and CRM fields.

Make the offer meaningful without allowing it to become an uncontrolled concession.

Step 5: Prepare the Sales Team

Representatives learn:

  • Who qualifies.
  • When to introduce the voucher.
  • How to describe it.
  • Which terms must be disclosed.
  • How to answer common questions.
  • When not to use the offer.
  • How to record it.

The voucher enters after fit and value are established.

Step 6: Present the Offer

An approved transition might be:

“For qualifying purchases during this promotion, we include a discounted travel voucher. May I show you exactly what it provides, what the recipient is responsible for, and which dates apply?”

The customer receives access to the actual terms before relying on the offer.

Step 7: Deliver and Redeem

The recipient receives the voucher through the approved delivery process, reviews the terms, completes activation, and follows the booking instructions.

Step 8: Measure the Sales Result

Track:

  • Eligible opportunities.
  • Offer presentation and acceptance.
  • Close rate.
  • Average discount and gross profit.
  • Time to close.
  • Cancellation and returns.
  • Customer questions and feedback.
  • Attributable revenue and commission.
