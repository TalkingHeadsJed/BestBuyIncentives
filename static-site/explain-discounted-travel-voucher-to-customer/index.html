How to Explain a Discounted Travel Voucher to a Customer

Learn a simple way to present a discounted travel voucher so customers understand the travel value, picture the experience, and feel motivated to act.

Schedule a Campaign Consultation

The best voucher presentation is simple, enthusiastic, and clear. It helps customers see the added value in their purchase and gives your team a natural reason to invite a decision.

Present it after the core purchase makes sense

Confirm fit, value, affordability, and authority first. Then use the discounted travel voucher when the buyer needs a final, exciting reason to stop waiting and choose the purchase that already makes sense.

Ask permission

“For qualifying purchases during this campaign, we include a discounted travel voucher. May I show you the travel experience it adds to your purchase?”

This opens the conversation around the added travel value before moving into the experience.

Explain the included travel value

Bring the travel experience to life: share the eligible travel category, destination options, nights or components, and occupancy using the current program language.

Give a clear overview of redemption

Walk the customer through the simple next steps, including registration, booking or redemption, and the travel choices they can make. Share the current program guide so they have everything in one place.

Make the experience easy to use

Provide the program guide and support contact so the recipient can plan confidently and enjoy the voucher.

Check understanding

Ask:

“What questions do you have about the travel experience or how to activate your voucher?”

Invite a real conversation so the customer leaves excited and informed.

Return to the decision

Once the customer sees the value, ask:

“With the discounted travel voucher included, are you ready to make this yours today?”

If it does, invite the customer to move forward. If they need more clarity, reconnect the conversation to the purchase value that matters most to them.

Give representatives approved materials

Use a polished one-page voucher guide, a manager escalation path, and CRM fields for presentation, issuance, objection, discount, and outcome.

Make the offer easy to choose

Review recipient travel voucher costs and how discounted travel vouchers work.
